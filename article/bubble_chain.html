How to play

Choose a topology and a starter layout, then click a bubble to begin. Bounded: bubbles that leave the canvas are removed. Torus (wrap): bubbles re-enter from the opposite edge; if nothing collides after one full lap, that moving bubble disappears. One click per attempt; use Reset attempt or Restart level manually.
