Procedural canvas puzzle

Water Sort

Arrange every bottle into a single color. Tap a bottle, then pour its whole top color into any bottle that still has room.

Your browser needs Canvas support to play Water Sort.

Tap or click a bottle to select it. Tap a second bottle to pour. With the board focused, number keys choose the matching bottle and Escape clears a selection. Controls below let you undo, restart, get a hint, view the best remaining solution, or make another seeded puzzle.

Select a bottle, then another bottle. Each pour moves the whole top color, or only what fits if the destination is nearly full.
